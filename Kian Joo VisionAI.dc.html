Recognising actual cleaning — what would be needed beyond PPE detection
TodayCleaning is inferred from the schedule. People detected at the area during the window are evidence of presence, not of cleaning. A supervisor confirms each session.
Cleaning-tool detectionAn object model trained on mops, brooms, scrubbers, hoses and buckets. A person with a tool in the zone suggests cleaning activity.
Action recognitionA pose or video-action model (sweeping, mopping, wiping) running on short clips rather than single frames. This needs clips and GPU time per camera.
Zone dwell + uniformDwell time in the cleaning zone plus a cleaning-crew uniform or apron attribute. This is cheaper, but only a proxy.
None of these is available in the current SenseStudio models. If one is added, its detections can mark a session "activity detected" in place of, or alongside, supervisor confirmation.
